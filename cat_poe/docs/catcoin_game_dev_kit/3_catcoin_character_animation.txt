CATCOIN RUNNER – CHARACTER ANIMATION BIBLE
==========================================

Art Direction
-------------
Use the official CatCoin mascot design language from the existing mining app.

Style
-----
2D cartoon
soft rounded shapes
expressive eyes
clean silhouette
mobile friendly readability

Technical Requirements
----------------------
PNG format
true transparent background
256x256 frame size

Required Animations
-------------------
Idle – 6 frames
Run – 12 frames
Turbo run – 12 frames
Jump – 6 frames
Double jump – 6 frames
Fall – 4 frames
Land – 5 frames
Coin collect – 6 frames
Damage – 6 frames
Victory – 8 frames

Run Cycle Specification
-----------------------
Frame 1 Contact
Front paw touches ground

Frame 2 Down
Body compresses

Frame 3 Gather start
Front legs fold inward

Frame 4 Full gather
All legs beneath body

Frame 5 Lift
Body extending

Frame 6 Front reach

Frame 7 Full extension

Frame 8 Flight

Frame 9 Rear contact

Frame 10 Push

Frame 11 Gather

Frame 12 Loop

All frames must maintain consistent character scale.
True transparency is mandatory.
