# Branding and trademarks

## Code license vs. brand

The **source code** in this repository is licensed under the terms of the `LICENSE` file in the root of the project (maintainers: ensure a license file is present and accurate).

The **project name**, logos, mascots, app store listings, and visual identity are **not** automatically licensed for reuse. They may be protected by trademark or other rights held by the project owners.

## Redistribution and forks

- You may fork and modify the code under the code license.
- **Forks and derivative apps** must not imply they are the official product unless explicitly authorized.
- **Remove or replace** official name, logo, icons, and store listing assets unless you have written permission to use them.
- **Community / self-hosted builds** must use their own backend, credentials, and branding so users are not confused with the official service.

## Official channels

Only deployments and app listings **explicitly published by the maintainers** represent the official product. Third-party servers or binaries are not endorsed unless stated otherwise.
